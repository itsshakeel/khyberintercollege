About the College

A Legacy of Discipline,
A Culture of Results

Khyber Inter College has served Kohat for over two decades, preparing students for board examinations, entry tests, and university life. Our approach is simple: small focused classes, regular testing, and teachers who genuinely know every student by name.

  • Weekly tests & monthly parent-teacher reviews
  • Dedicated MDCAT / ECAT preparation support
  • Separate, secure and disciplined campus environment
  • Affordable fee structure with merit & need-based concessions

Admissions 2026–27

Your Seat is Waiting

Four simple steps. Seats are limited per section — early applicants get priority and scholarship test eligibility.

1

Apply

Fill the form below or visit campus with your documents.

2

Scholarship Test

Appear in the entry test to unlock up to 100% merit concession.

3

Interview

4

Enroll

Confirm your seat, collect books & join orientation week.

Required Documents

  • Matric result card (or hope certificate)
  • B-Form / CNIC copy of student & father
  • 4 passport-size photographs
  • School leaving certificate
